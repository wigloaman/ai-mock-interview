Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

What we store

When you create an account we store your name, email address and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text). If you fill in your profile we also store the details you add, such as your location, job role, company and LinkedIn or GitHub links.

For each mock interview we store the job role, company and job description you enter, the questions asked, your answers, and the AI-generated review. Messages sent through Contact Us and responses to the feedback form are also stored.

Services that process your data

  • Groq generates interview questions, follow-up questions and reviews from your interview details and answers, and converts recorded voice answers into text.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech converts interview questions into spoken audio. Only the question text is sent.
  • Supabase hosts the database where the information above is stored.

Voice recordings are only used to produce the text of your answer and are not stored by this site. Camera video, if you turn it on, stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run your mock interviews, show you your results and past interviews, and reply to messages you send. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time. To have your account and interview history deleted, send a request through the Contact Us page.
